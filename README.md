# UK Gender Pay Gap Dashboard (Power BI)

A self-initiated Power BI dashboard analysing three years of UK government gender pay gap reporting.

## Business question
How large is the UK gender pay gap, how has it changed over time and which industries have the biggest and smallest gaps?

## Data
- 33,680 employer gender pay gap reports over 3 years, from UK government open data
- Employer SIC codes mapped to 21 industry sections

## What I did
- Cleaned and combined the data in **Power Query**
- Mapped SIC codes to 21 industry sections
- Wrote **DAX** measures for the median pay gap and the share of employers paying men more
- Built the dashboard in **Power BI**

## Key findings
- The UK median gender pay gap fell only from **8.9% to 8.0%** in two years
- The gap is widest in **Education, Construction and Finance**
- Women were under-represented in the top-paid quarter in **all 18 industries** analysed (industries with fewer than 50 employers and employers with no industry stated, were excluded to keep the comparison reliable)
